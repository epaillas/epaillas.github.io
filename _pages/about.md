I am an astronomer and cosmologist based in Santiago de Chile, working as an Assistant Professor at the [Instituto de Estudios Astrofísicos](https://astronomia.udp.cl) of Universidad Diego Portales. My research focuses on observational cosmology, combining theory and observations to learn about the nature of dark energy, the growth of cosmic structure, and the physics of the early Universe.
 
I obtained my PhD in astrophysics from [Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile](https://www.uc.cl), working under the supervision of [Nelson Padilla](https://iate.oac.uncor.edu/en/staff-members/2544/). I then worked as a postdoctoral fellow at the [Waterloo Centre for Astrophysics](https://uwaterloo.ca/astrophysics-centre/), in Ontario, Canada, and at the [Cosmology Lab of Steward Observatory](https://cosmolab.arizona.edu), in Tucson, Arizona.

I am an active member of the [DESI](https://www.desi.lbl.gov) Collaboration, where I co-chair the Galaxy & Quasar Clustering working group. I also participate in the [LSST Dark Energy Science Collaboration](https://lsstdesc.org), where I am interested in exploring weak lensing and supernovae science. You can click [here](https://astronomia.udp.cl/en/research/areas/cosmology-and-lss/) to learn about research opportunities.
